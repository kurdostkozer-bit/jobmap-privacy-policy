JobMap - Delete Account & Data Request

We respect your privacy and provide full control over your personal data. If you wish to delete your JobMap account and all associated personal information, you can do so easily.

How to Request Account Deletion:

Option 1: In-App Deletion

  • Open the JobMap app.
  • Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account.
  • Confirm the deletion request.

Option 2: Direct Email Request

If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion by contacting us via email:

  • Email: kurdostkozer@gmail.com
  • Subject: Account Deletion Request - JobMap
  • Details to include: Your registered email address or username.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

Upon receiving your request, the following data will be permanently removed from our active databases within 7 days:

  • Your profile information (Name, Email, Phone number).
  • Your CV, job applications, and saved jobs.
  • Your message history and account preferences.

Note: Certain transaction logs or compliance records may be retained for a limited period if required by law or for fraud prevention.
