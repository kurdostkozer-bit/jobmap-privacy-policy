Privacy Policy - JobMap

Effective Date: August 12, 2026
Last Updated: August 12, 2026

1. Introduction

JobMap ("we", "our", or "the App") is a job-search and recruitment platform that connects job seekers with employers. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use JobMap.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Profile information
  • Employment history and professional experience
  • Education, qualifications, and skills
  • Resume / CV information
  • Job applications and saved jobs
  • Location information, when you choose to provide it
  • Messages and communications between users
  • Payment and transaction information when payment features are used
  • Device, technical, and diagnostic information
  • Information about how you use the App

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and operate JobMap services
  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Help job seekers discover relevant employment opportunities
  • Allow employers to publish and manage job listings
  • Process job applications
  • Enable communication between users
  • Provide notifications and service updates
  • Process and manage payments where applicable
  • Improve the App and its features
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security issues
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements

4. How We Share Information

We may share information when necessary to provide the services you request. For example:

  • Job seeker profile and application information may be shared with employers for jobs to which the user applies.
  • Employer job information may be shown to job seekers.
  • Messages may be visible to the participants of the relevant conversation.
  • We may use trusted service providers to operate infrastructure, authentication, hosting, analytics, communications, and payment services.
  • Information may be disclosed when required by law or to protect the security and rights of users and the service.

We do not sell users' personal information to third parties.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect user information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Communications with our services may be protected using HTTPS/TLS encryption.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services, maintain accounts, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

When an account is deleted, we will delete or anonymize personal information where reasonably possible, subject to information that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or accounting purposes.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and personal information
  • Control optional location permissions
  • Manage notification preferences
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

8. Account Deletion

Users may request deletion of their account and associated personal information through the available account settings or by contacting us.

9. Children's Privacy

JobMap is intended for users who are legally able to use employment services. The App is not directed toward children under the applicable minimum age for using such services.

10. Third-Party Services

JobMap may rely on third-party services for infrastructure, authentication, communications, analytics, and payment processing. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable agreements.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, we will update the "Last Updated" date shown on this page.
